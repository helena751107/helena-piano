# Helena Piano — a classical webzine on one phone

![Helena Piano — a classical webzine rendered on one phone](assets/social-preview.jpg)

> A high-end classical-music webzine, rendered and served from an old Galaxy S21:
> piano music produced on-device (tech), plus listening, reviews, and interviews (magazine).
>
> Part of the [**Helena Content Foundry**](https://github.com/helena751107/helena_phone) — mobile-first, MCP-verified.

---

## Two lanes

| Lane | What | Where |
|------|------|-------|
| **Lane 1 · IT rendering** | The pipeline that makes piano music on an S21 | `bgm/` — 12 MIDI pieces → fluidsynth + Salamander Grand Piano → MP3 |
| **Lane 2 · Magazine** | Listening · reviews · interviews · theory · scores | Classical / piano articles |

## SSOT (single source of truth)

The source content lives **here (this GitHub repo)** — it is the original. Tistory is the display / inbound copy.

# Helena Piano — 폰 한 대로 만드는 클래식 웹진

> **구형 S21에서 피아노 음악을 직접 렌더링하고, 그 위에 클래식 웹진을 올린다.**
> 명품 음악 잡지의 디자인 언어를 참고한 **하이엔드 클래식 웹진**.

## 두 갈래(레인)

| 레인 | 무엇 | 위치 |
|------|------|------|
| **레인 1 · IT 렌더링** | S21에서 피아노 음악을 만드는 파이프라인 | `bgm/` — MIDI 12곡 → fluidsynth + Salamander Grand Piano로 MP3 렌더 |
