01Studio pillars

Four zones · mini studio

Tap a card and that zone's meter fills in. The practice room and the broadcast desk share one roof.

01 · Practice

Practice

The zone that keeps hands, ears, and time. A log of repetition, not perfection.

  • Session time · tempo notes
  • Marking sticking bars
  • Runs alongside the S21 timer / metronome
Focus90%
02 · Repertoire

Repertoire

What's going up now, what's coming next. The list is the broadcast plan.

  • Piece cards · difficulty
  • Public vs. practice-only split
  • Can cross with the Faith axis
Focus82%
03 · Record

Record

Capturing clips. One phone is enough for a mini-session take.

  • Audio / video takes
  • File naming rules
  • termux-setup · youtube/
Focus86%
04 · Broadcast

Broadcast

The last door out, onto YouTube. Short or long.

  • Shorts / full-length performances
  • Title & thumbnail drafts (_Grok)
  • Connects to the hub funnel
